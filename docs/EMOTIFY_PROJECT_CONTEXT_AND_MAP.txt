3. AUTHORITATIVE DATA MODEL (KEY CONVEX TABLES)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. users:
   - _id, clerkId, full_name, mobile_number, role ("patient"|"counsellor"|"admin"), status ("active"|"inactive"),
     patientId (e.g. STU-001), emergencyContactName, emergencyContactPhone, mitraPreferences.
2. screeningAttempts:
   - Authoritative questionnaire intake records.
   - instrumentVersions, responses (phq9, gad7, pq16, wsas, reqol10), results (scores, flags, severities).
   - attemptType: "baseline" | "periodic" | "followup".
3. triages:
   - Authoritative clinical risk tier.
   - level: "green" | "amber" | "red", suicideFlag: boolean, psychosisFlag: boolean, attemptId.
4. counsellorAlerts:
   - Critical clinical alerts requiring counselor action.
   - type: "suicideRisk" | "severeDepression" | "psychosisRisk" | "manual_sos" | "triageEscalation".
   - status: "pending" | "acknowledged" | "resolved".
5. cbtSessions:
   - Interactive, stateful CBT cognitive restructuring session.
   - status: "active" | "completed" | "abandoned" | "safety_mode" | "expired".
   - currentStep: "understanding" | "clarification" | "guided_discovery" | "belief_challenge" |
                  "balanced_thought" | "emotion_rating" | "recovery_coach" | "completed".
   - conversation: message history.
   - bridges automatically and idempotently to reframeLogs on completion.
6. reframeLogs:
   - Canonical historical saved cognitive reframes.
   - situation_text, thought_original, thinking_trap_choice, guided_answers, reframe_text,
     pre_reframe_intensity, post_reframe_intensity, improvement_percentage, cbtSessionId.
7. jpmrLogs:
   - Somatic Jacobson's Progressive Muscle Relaxation records.
   - preIntensity (1-10), postIntensity (1-10), durationSeconds, completed: boolean,
     sourceType ("self_initiated" | "routine" | "screening" | "triage"), attemptId, triageId.
8. microGoals:
   - Non-clinical daily habits & challenge goals.
   - goalId, goalTitle, goalDescription, category, difficulty, points, completed, status ("pending"|"completed"|"skipped").
9. jpmrVideos:
   - Media storage references for 15-step somatic video guidance.
   - stepIndex, title, storageId (Id<"_storage">). Admin-only mutations.

4. CRUCIAL ARCHITECTURAL INVARIANTS & CLINICAL GUARDRAILS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Absolute Clinical Decoupling:
   - Clinical screening scores (PHQ-9, GAD-7, PQ-16, WSAS, ReQoL-10) and triage tiers (green/amber/red)
     MUST NEVER be used as inputs for ordinary intervention recommendations (daily habits, CBT reframes, JPMR).
   - Provenance fields (sourceType, attemptId, triageId) exist strictly for clinical audit and counselor timeline.
2. Non-Fabrication Rule:
   - If an intervention was self-initiated or direct, attemptId and triageId MUST remain undefined.
   - Never query "latest screening" to fabricate provenance on an unlinked session.
3. Server-Authoritative Identity:
   - Every mutation reads userId from `identity.subject` via `ctx.auth.getUserIdentity()`.
   - Client-provided userId arguments in mutations (e.g. `userId`) are strictly ignored or rejected.
4. Deterministic Habit Engine:
   - Daily routine micro-goals are chosen deterministically from `common/interventions.ts`
     using user ID + calendar date hash. Zero `Math.random` in production habit selection.
5. Reframe Architecture:
   - `cbtSessions` = active state machine.
   - `reframeLogs` = permanent saved reframe history.
   - Bridge from CBT session to `reframeLogs` is idempotent via the `by_cbtSessionId` index.
6. Crisis & Safety Interception:
   - In CBT or companion, any explicit suicidal ideation or self-harm keywords trigger immediate
     server-authoritative `safety_mode`, generate a `suicideRisk` alert for counselors,
     and show the 988 Crisis Lifeline. Sessions in `safety_mode` never bridge to ordinary `reframeLogs`.
